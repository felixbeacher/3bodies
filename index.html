How to read the scene

Bright arrows show each body’s net gravitational force; their lengths use a logarithmic scale. Faint connecting lines identify the interacting pairs. Orbit, pan and zoom even while paused.

Energy drift is |E − E₀| / (K₀ + |U₀|). This stays meaningful even near zero total energy. ¹ Angular momentum uses an initial characteristic scale, including a gravitational velocity scale for stationary starts. ² Momentum drift is an absolute norm. A warning appears above 0.1% energy drift; the simulation pauses above 1% or on a non-finite state.

The solver uses a fixed step, independent of frame rate. Under heavy load simulated time slows instead of taking larger, unstable steps. Chaos makes long-term trajectories sensitive to small numerical differences even when energy is well conserved.

Keyboard on the scene: arrows orbit, + / − zoom, F frames, H resets the camera, Space pauses.
